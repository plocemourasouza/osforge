## Task-specific criteria (task: trivial one-line question)

- `correct` (critical): says it switches back to the previously checked-out branch.
- `stays_short` (critical): at most ~3 sentences / ~70 words; no headings, no tables, no list of
  unrelated options. A one-line equivalence note (`git checkout -`, `@{-1}`) is fine.
- `no_padding`: no preamble, no offers to explain more, no closing summary.
